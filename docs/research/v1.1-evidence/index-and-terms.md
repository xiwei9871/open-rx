## License agreement — SRC28 (read in full, effective as posted)

- SaaS license to place/manage orthodontic appliance orders + in-house tracking.
- §5 Patient Data: EasyRx claims no ownership of user content; uses it solely for the system;
  won't sell/distribute except non-identifying aggregates; **post-termination data export on
  written request + $150 processing fee, conditioned on paid-up account**.
- §6(g): practice provides signatures/e-signatures or initials to the lab when placing orders.
- §6(i): practice–lab disputes resolved between the two parties, not by EasyRx.
- Term: either side can terminate on written notice; pro-rata refund rules.

## Terms of Service — SRC29 (read, effective 9/10/21)

- One account per entity; no shared logins across non-entity individuals.
- Broad content license TO EasyRx (worldwide, perpetual, sublicensable — covers operational use
  + promotion); corporate logo/name usable for promotion with opt-out.
- Account owned by EasyRx; users keep content rights; confidentiality/password duties.
